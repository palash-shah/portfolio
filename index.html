About

Turning noisy signals into calibrated forecasts.

I'm Palash Shah, a student at Carnegie Mellon University. Good forecasting is a craft: break a question down, weigh the evidence honestly, and put a number on it you're willing to be scored against. I got hooked on that discipline through Metaculus forecasting tournaments, where every prediction eventually meets reality and your track record is the only thing that talks.

Most of my work sits where machine learning meets human judgment: systems that ingest news and data, aggregate many weak signals into one estimate, and stay honest about their own uncertainty. I care as much about a forecast being calibrated as about it being sharp — a confident wrong answer is worse than an honest maybe.
